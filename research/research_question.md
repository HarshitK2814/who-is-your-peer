# Research Question

Two SEC filings from the Vanguard Quantitative Equity Group state that **industry/sector bets do not
add value** (2020 N-CSR: "sector bets do not add value over the long term"; 2019 N-CSR: minimising
"risks that our research indicates don't improve returns (such as industry selection…)"). The 2026
prospectus commits only to **beta** neutrality and allows the advisor to "emphasize specific industries".

**RQ1.** For a five-theme composite (growth, quality, management decisions, sentiment, valuation),
does the *between-peer-group* component (the industry bet) earn a positive risk-adjusted return,
net of costs, in a beta-neutral long/short book?

**RQ2.** Does the peer definition matter? Do finer (SIC-3) or statistical (return-correlation cluster)
peers give a better within-peer composite than the conventional Fama–French 49 industries?

**RQ3 (design).** Does adding explicit industry neutrality to a peer-relative composite
(sector-neutral, as in 2020) change risk-adjusted performance relative to beta-only neutrality (as in 2026)?

Formal hypotheses, metrics and failure criteria: `preregistration.md` (frozen before the test period).
